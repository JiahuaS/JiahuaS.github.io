Reduced-order modeling

Data-Driven Reduced-Order Model for Radiative Transfer Equation

Supervisor: Kui Ren  ·  Collaborators: Yinxi Pan, Shanyin Tong

This project develops reduced-order models for the radiative transfer equation, focusing on angular-averaged local density rather than the full phase-space solution. Starting from an integral formulation of isotropic transport, we construct a data-driven reduced basis from full-order simulations using upwind discretization, discrete ordinates, and source iteration. An SVD-based offline–online model efficiently approximates solutions for new source functions, with encouraging results in one- and two-dimensional experiments.

Operator learning

Operator Learning for Quasi-Linear Hyperbolic Systems

Supervisor: Kui Ren

This project explores solution-operator learning for quasi-linear hyperbolic PDEs. Motivated by Roe linearization and characteristic decomposition, it learns Roe-average states and characteristic variables, and combines physics-informed and Fourier neural operator components to solve decoupled characteristic equations. The aim is structure-aware operator learning for systems with smooth or discontinuous initial data.

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for PDE Discovery

Supervisor: Sharon Di

In this project I studied identifying governing PDEs from data using physics-informed neural networks and symbolic regression. I proposed the Nontrivial Transformation Method to uncover hidden relations among candidate PDE expressions under suitable transformations. The approach combines PINN-based modeling with PEQL and SGA-PDE and was explored using benchmark problems including the heat equation.

Numerical analysis

Stability Analysis of Linear Multistep Methods

Supervisor: Qiang Du

This project examines stability of time-integration schemes for large ODE systems arising in scientific computing and semidiscretized PDEs. Inspired by numerical-range-based stability theory for Runge–Kutta methods ↗, I investigated a partial extension of related techniques to linear multistep methods, with the longer-term goal of dimension-independent stability criteria.
